FastTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FastTrack (the “App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. FastTrack is an intermittent-fasting tracker that helps you run fasting timers and log basic health metrics. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We only collect the data you provide while using the App:

  • Account information — your email address and a display name, used to create and secure your account and to sync your data across devices.
  • Fasting activity — your fasting sessions (start time, planned goal, actual end time, completion status, duration) and fasting streaks.
  • Health metrics you enter — optional daily figures such as water intake, weight, calories, and step count.
  • App preferences — your selected fasting plan, theme, units, and notification settings.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising. The App contains no advertising and uses no third-party analytics or tracking services.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: running your fasting timer, tracking streaks, and showing your history.
  • To sync your data so it is available when you sign in on another device.
  • To send local device notifications for fasting milestones and reminders you enable.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of your account.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored locally on your device and synced to our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and handles authentication on our behalf. Supabase processes this data solely to provide the App's functionality. Data in transit is protected using encryption (HTTPS/TLS), and access to stored data is restricted by row-level security so each user can only access their own records.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the App, which removes your associated data from our systems. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

FastTrack is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Intermittent fasting may not be appropriate for minors; the App is intended for adults.

Health Disclaimer

FastTrack is a tracking and informational tool only. It does not provide medical advice and is not a substitute for professional healthcare. Consult a qualified health provider before starting any fasting routine, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, or have a medical condition.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, by an in-app notice.
